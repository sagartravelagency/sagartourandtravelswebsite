About Sagar Tour and Travels

Lucknow's trusted travel partner since 2014 — serving families, pilgrims, corporates, and wedding celebrations with reliable, transparent taxi and travel services.

Our Story

Sagar Tour and Travels was founded in 2014 in Jankipuram, Lucknow, with a simple mission: to provide Lucknow's families and travelers with a taxi service they could truly trust — one with transparent pricing, well-maintained vehicles, and professional drivers who treat every passenger like family.

Starting with just a handful of sedans, we've grown to a fleet of 50+ vehicles covering every category from economy sedans to luxury Mercedes and BMW. Today, we serve over 15,000 happy customers across local city rides, outstation trips to Ayodhya, Varanasi, Agra, and Delhi, premium wedding car rentals, and corporate travel solutions.

What sets us apart isn't just our fleet or our rates — it's the trust we've built through a decade of honest, reliable service. We display our real pricing (no "Price on Request"), our vehicles are genuine (no stock photos), and our drivers are vetted, trained professionals who know Lucknow and UP inside out.
