Lucknow to Ayodhya — Route Details

The Lucknow to Ayodhya route is one of the most popular pilgrimage routes in Uttar Pradesh. The holy city of Ayodhya, the birthplace of Lord Ram, attracts millions of devotees every year, especially since the completion of the Ram Mandir.

Route Highlights

  • Ram Janmabhoomi Temple — The grand Ram Mandir, the primary destination for most travelers
  • Hanuman Garhi — Ancient temple dedicated to Lord Hanuman, located on a hill
  • Kanak Bhawan — Beautiful temple known for its ornate interior
  • Saryu River Ghat — Sacred river ghats for evening aarti

Best Route

The fastest route is via NH-27 (Lucknow-Ayodhya Highway). The road is well-maintained and typically takes 3-3.5 hours depending on traffic. Our experienced drivers know the best routes and stops along the way.

Recommended Vehicle

For families of 4-5, a Sedan (₹12/km) or SUV (₹15/km) is ideal. For larger groups visiting multiple temples, we recommend an Innova Crysta (₹18/km) for extra comfort and luggage space.
